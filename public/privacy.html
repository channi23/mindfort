Mindfort

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Mindfort is a free educational memory-palace website. This page explains how the current version handles information.

Content stored on your device

Your fortresses, drawings, memory notes, recall responses and review history are stored in your browser's local storage. In the current version, Mindfort does not require accounts and does not operate a server that receives those notes. Clearing browser data, using a different device, or private-browsing sessions may remove this information. Export a backup before clearing data.

Hosting and third-party resources

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser requests for security and service operation. The editor uses the Excalidraw open-source package; other interface resources may be loaded from third-party content delivery services.

Analytics and advertising

Mindfort does not currently load Google AdSense ads or an advertising tracking service. If advertising or analytics are introduced, this notice will be updated to describe the services and any required consent choices before they are enabled.

Sharing and exports

Exported JSON backups contain your learning content. They are created on your device; do not share backups unless you are comfortable sharing the contents.

Questions

For site-related questions, open an issue in the Mindfort GitHub repository. Do not post sensitive personal information in public issues.
